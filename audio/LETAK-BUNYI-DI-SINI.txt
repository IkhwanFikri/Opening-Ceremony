## Add Background Music & Sound Effects (Optional)

Place the MP3 audio files in the `audio` folder using the following filenames:

- **`background.mp3`** — Background music that plays continuously throughout the inauguration gimmick.
- **`soundeffect.mp3`** — Short sound effect played each time a puzzle piece is correctly placed.
- **`closing.mp3`** — Closing music or sound effect played when the puzzle is completed and the inauguration sequence ends.

### Important Instructions
- All audio files must be in MP3 format.
- Filenames must be lowercase and match the names above exactly.
- Update the JavaScript audio paths to match these filenames.
- Missing audio files should fall back to the original synthetic sounds.
- Background music should loop continuously and fade out smoothly when the puzzle is completed.
- The closing audio should play at the appropriate final stage of the inauguration sequence.
