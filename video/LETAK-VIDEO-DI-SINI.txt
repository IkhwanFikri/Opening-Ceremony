Place the inauguration video in this folder using the following filename:

    gimicvideo.mp4

The video will automatically play in full screen approximately 4 seconds after the puzzle is completed and the poster is displayed in full screen.

Press **P** on your keyboard to play or replay the video.

**Tips:** Use MP4 format (H.264), with a recommended resolution of 1920 × 1080. When uploading through the GitHub website, the file size must be under 25 MB.
